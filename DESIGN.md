## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an evocative nocturnal luxury inspired by ancient Nile civilization and modern Madrid mixology culture. The aesthetic bridges historical opulence with modern nightlife sophistication: geometric Art Deco patterns, subtle hieroglyphic precision, low-lit terrace ambiances, and architectural balance.

The visual style blends **Glassmorphism** with **Deep Tonal Layering** and **Fine Metallic Accents**. Dark charcoal canvases evoke warm Madrid summer nights, while luminous old gold and mysterious deep teal highlights introduce an aura of ceremony and VIP exclusivity. Interfaces feel tactile, velvet-soft, and illuminated like back-lit onyx bar counters.

## Colors

The palette relies entirely on deep nocturnal surfaces punctuated by gilded highlights and submerged mineral accents.

- **Foundational Surfaces**:
  - `Base Canvas`: `#0E0E10` (Charcoal Black) — absolute background depth.
  - `Layer 1 Surface`: `#16161A` (Deep Night) — layout containers, drawers, and elevated sections.
  - `Card / Surface Glass`: `#1C1C22` (Graphite Sheen) at 70% to 85% opacity with dynamic back-filtering.
- **Primary Gilded Scale**:
  - `Primary Gold (Base)`: `#C8A15A` — brand focal points, borders, active states, key icons.
  - `Warm Gold (Hover/Focus)`: `#DFB76C` — interactive state transitions.
  - `Golden Glow (Ambient)`: `#F4D38E` — text emphasizes, inner glow sources, radiant micro-highlights.
- **Atmospheric Accents**:
  - `Nile Emerald / Teal`: `#0E3A3B` — reserved for VIP markers, hookah smoke overlays, water features, and rich accent badges.
  - `Warm Amber`: `#D97724` — used selectively for warmth, fire, signature spicy cocktails, and status indicators.
- **Typography & Details**:
  - `Text High-Contrast`: `#F5F5F7` (Crisp Warm White) — optimal legibility across dim lounge settings.
  - `Text Secondary`: `#9E9AA0` (Muted Gold-Gray) — metadata, volume measurements, auxiliary descriptors.
  - `Gilded Rim Border`: `rgba(200, 161, 90, 0.2)` — hairline structure definition.

## Typography

The type system creates deliberate dramatic tension between classical architectural serifs and ultra-clean modern geometric letterforms:

- **Headlines & Display (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys timeless Mediterranean and Nilotic grandeur with high-contrast serifs, razor-sharp terminals, and an editorial rhythm. Used for cocktail names, category titles, and experiential hero copy. All uppercase headline variations must receive wider tracking (`0.06em` to `0.1em`).
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Neutral, ultra-legible, and balanced with open apertures to maintain maximum readability on mobile devices used under low-light lounge circumstances.
- **Labels & Overlines**: Rendered in uppercase with pronounced letter-spacing to emulate fine brass and stone inscriptions.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict **8pt base spacing rhythm** paired with a responsive fluid grid.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Mobile`: `< 768px` (4-column layout, 16px gutters, 16px outer safety margins).
  - `Tablet`: `768px - 1024px` (8-column layout, 24px gutters, 32px outer margins).
  - `Desktop`: `> 1024px` (12-column layout, max content boundary `1280px`, 24px gutters, dynamic margins).

Horizontal distribution relies on generous negative space around signature offerings (such as reservation flow, author cocktails, and private shisha selections). Dense vertical stacks are restricted strictly to ingredients and technical shisha blend notes.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered semi-translucent substrates, golden ambient backlights, and precision-etched borders rather than opaque drop shadows.

- **Base Canvas (`#0E0E10`)**: Deep void base with subtle, localized radial gradients of `#0E3A3B` (at 12% opacity) simulating low floor spotlights.
- **Level 1 (Sections & Backdrops)**: Solid `#16161A` or 85% opacity `#16161A` with a `16px` backdrop-blur filter.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Drawers)**: `#1C1C22` at 70% opacity, `24px` backdrop-filter blur, enclosed by a `1px` continuous border in `rgba(200, 161, 90, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Menus)**: `#1C1C22` at 92% opacity, encased in a dual-line stroke illusion: `1px solid rgba(200, 161, 90, 0.35)` with an ambient cast shadow: `0 20px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 24px rgba(200, 161, 90, 0.12)`.
- **Golden Aura (Light Flares)**: Interactive elements, when active or focused, emit an inner radial glow: `box-shadow: inset 0 0 12px rgba(244, 211, 142, 0.15), 0 0 16px rgba(200, 161, 90, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **pill-shaped geometry (`roundedness: 3`)** for high-interaction objects (action triggers, tags, category pills, table selection markers), contrasting elegantly with refined, architectural rectangles for image frames and narrative editorial sections.

- **Pill Elements**: Radius `9999px` used for buttons, interactive badges, segmented controls, and search inputs.
- **Card Surfaces**: Radius `24px` (`rounded-xl` equivalent) for containers, cocktail cards, and reservation modals.
- **Sub-elements & Media**: Radius `16px` (`rounded-lg` equivalent) for cocktail imagery, map containers, and inner dialog content.
- **Emblem Framing**: Geometric octagons and lozenges derived from Art Deco geometry serve as outer bounds for badge icons and the lion insignia.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (VIP Reservation, Order)**: Full pill geometry. Background is a linear gradient from Old Gold `#C8A15A` to Warm Gold `#DFB76C`. Text rendered in `#0E0E10` using `label-lg` uppercase typography. Active state elevates into an outer glow of `0 0 20px rgba(244, 211, 142, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary Action (Explore Flavors, View Terrace)**: Full pill geometry. Transparent glass fill with `1px` stroke of `#C8A15A`. Text rendered in `#F5F5F7`. Hover initiates background tint of `rgba(200, 161, 90, 0.12)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Borderless, crisp warm white `#F5F5F7` text with a golden line micro-indicator that expands outwards on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Category selectors (e.g., "Signature Cocktails", "Exclusive Shisha", "Tapas & Bites") use fully rounded pill shapes.
- Unselected: Dark glass (`rgba(28, 28, 34, 0.6)`) with muted text (`#9E9AA0`) and hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Selected: Deep Nile Teal (`#0E3A3B`) core with a bright golden border (`#C8A15A`) and crisp gold text (`#F4D38E`).

### Cards & Menu Listings
- Cards use frosted dark glass (`#1C1C22` at 75% opacity) with `24px` rounded corners and a hairline perimeter border (`rgba(200, 161, 90, 0.2)`).
- Card titles utilize `headline-sm` in `Playfair Display`. Botanical notes, hookah strength ratings, or cocktail notes sit beneath in `body-sm` (`#9E9AA0`).
- Prices are styled in `headline-sm` utilizing the radiant gold `#F4D38E`.

### Inputs & Date-Time Pickers
- Input bars (e.g., party size, reservation date, guest name) use pill shapes or soft `16px` rounded surfaces filled with `#16161A`.
- Placeholder text sits in `#9E9AA0`. Focus state creates a sharp `1px` border of `#DFB76C` and an ambient diffuse glow.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Selection indicators are customized as circular rings (radios) or softened lozenges (checkboxes).
- Unchecked: Inset border of `rgba(200, 161, 90, 0.3)`.
- Checked: Solid `#0E3A3B` filled center with an inlaid `#DFB76C` geometric dot or checkmark.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Hookah Intensity Scale**: A 5-point progression track of stylized golden lotus/deco dots indicating flavor potency and cooling levels.
- **Terrace Table Map**: Interactive architectural map using dark `#16161A` floors with `#C8A15A` glowing outlines for available lounge booths and open-sky terrace seats.
